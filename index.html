REGIME. Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 8, 2026

REGIME ("the app") is built to be private by design.

What we collect

REGIME does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, health data, habit data, advertising identifier, or payment details. To provide and validate paid access, our purchase-management provider RevenueCat receives App Store purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. We do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in REGIME — your non-negotiables, streaks, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. RevenueCat validates receipts, manages entitlements, and provides aggregated purchase analytics. We never receive or store your payment-card information. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Third parties

REGIME uses RevenueCat for purchase validation, entitlement management, and purchase analytics. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier for these purposes. REGIME uses no advertising frameworks and does not sell personal data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
