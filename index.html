Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

REGIME is a privacy-first habit tracker. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The App Store privacy label for REGIME is Data Not Collected.

Who we are

REGIME: Daily Habit Tracker is published by Ilias Aberkane (“we”, “us”). Apple ID: 6805791943. Bundle ID: com.iliasaberkane.regime. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch (iOS 18.0+ / iPadOS 18.0+ / watchOS 11.0+).

What REGIME stores

  • On your device by default: your habit list, check-ins, streak, ranks, and settings stay on your device. REGIME does not require an account and does not run its own cloud service for habit entries.
  • Optional iCloud sync (v1.1+): if you turn sync on, habit data can leave the device through your own iCloud account. If sync is off, habit entries stay on-device. We do not claim data “never leaves the device” when sync is enabled.
  • No REGIME account: there is no REGIME login, profile, or social feed.
  • Optional reminders: morning or evening reminders use iOS local notifications on your device.
  • Optional sharing: if you share a completed-day milestone, you choose the destination through the system share sheet.

Purchases

After onboarding, habit tracking requires a subscription or lifetime unlock. Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may share limited purchase status with us (for example, whether a subscription is active) so the app can unlock features. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Current options shown on the App Store: Weekly $4.99 · Yearly $29.99 · Lifetime $49.99. Yearly includes a free trial when Apple shows an eligible introductory offer. There is no 3-day trial.

Purchase validation (RevenueCat)

To provide and validate paid access, our purchase-management provider RevenueCat receives App Store purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. We do not use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking. We never receive or store your payment-card information.

Analytics and tracking

The App Store privacy label for REGIME is Data Not Collected. We do not sell your personal information. Purchase-related signals from Apple or StoreKit are used only to run entitlements (restore purchases, unlock features).

Children

REGIME is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13. Age rating on the App Store: 4+.
