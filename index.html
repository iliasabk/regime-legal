REGIME. Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

REGIME ("the app") is built to be private by design.

What we collect

Nothing. REGIME does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in REGIME — your non-negotiables, streaks, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Third parties

REGIME uses no third-party SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no analytics providers.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date.
