A consistency tracker is a record of attendance, and attendance here has one definition. You showed up on the days the checklist reached a DAY COMPLETE stamp. You did not show up on the days it did not. REGIME keeps that record as one streak, also called the chain, and lays the last seven days out when you open the weekly review.

A hard day that finished counts. A day you only planned does not.

One chain, updated once a day

The streak grows when every non-negotiable is done. Miss the day and the streak resets. You can begin again the next morning. A single finished line cannot hold the number up while the rest of the list is still open, because the chain belongs to the whole checklist.

Picture a plain week. Monday gets the stamp. Tuesday gets the stamp. Wednesday leaves the training line open at midnight, so Wednesday has no stamp and the streak returns to the start. Thursday begins a new chain. When you review the week, you see two stamps, a gap, and whatever Thursday through the weekend earned after that. The gap stays a gap. The explanation you have for Wednesday is not stored next to it.

The case for leaving that gap visible is on why a hard streak. The same rule told as a chain you refuse to snap is on don't break the chain.

The stamp is today's mark

Hold each habit to commit it after the work. That hold is how a line becomes done. When the last line is held, the day receives a DAY COMPLETE stamp. The stamp is the consistency mark. Opening the app, rearranging the order, and thinking about a habit leave the day blank.

If you want that stamp on an ordinary Tuesday, the list has to fit an ordinary Tuesday. Three lines you finish will mark more weeks than twelve lines you negotiate. Deciding which lines deserve to be on the list is the work on daily non-negotiables.

Read seven days before you rebuild anything

The weekly review is the last seven days of check-ins. Count the stamps before you count your intentions. Seven stamps means you showed up seven times. Four stamps means you showed up four times, including on the days that felt busy and still did not finish. Read the empty dates first.

Then change one thing. Move a line to an hour you actually have, or drop a line you did not touch. Leave every other line where it is until the next review. Replacing the whole checklist after one thin week turns consistency back into planning, and planning does not get a stamp.

The names can wait

Eight ranks, from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL, sit on this same chain. Each name's day count is written on the habit tracker with ranks page. You do not need a new name in order to show up tomorrow. You need the list, the hold, and the stamp. Looking at the week with nobody else in the room is the self accountability page.

You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device. Share a milestone if you want a picture of one finished day. The stamp is already the record.

FAQ

What counts as showing up?
A finished checklist. The day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp after every line has been held to commit. An opened list is not a finished day.
Is there one streak or one per habit?
One streak for the whole day. The chain grows when the checklist is done and resets when you miss a day.
What do I do with the weekly review?
Read the last seven days. Count the days with a stamp. Change one line the empty days already point at, then leave the list alone until the next week.
Do the ranks replace the daily mark?
No. Ranks name the length of the chain. The daily mark is still the DAY COMPLETE stamp. The day counts for the ranks are on the ranks page.
Do I need an account?
No. Showing up is recorded on your device. There is no REGIME account.
