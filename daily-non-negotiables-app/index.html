A daily non-negotiable is a task the day is incomplete without. People looking for a daily non negotiables list, for examples, or for an app are trying to stop rewriting the plan every morning. Keep it short enough to finish on a tired day.

What belongs on the list

Ask one question of each line: if I skip this, did I still do the day I said I would do? If the answer is yes, leave it off. A to-do list collects work. A non-negotiables list refuses anything that is not part of the minimum. A long list feels serious and then collapses. Repeatable beats impressive.

Example lists you can type yourself

These are examples, not a catalog inside REGIME. Type the lines that match your life. If a line would be a lie by Thursday, leave it off.

  • Fitness. One training session. A walk outside. One meal you decided in advance. In bed by the time you wrote down.
  • Study. One study sitting with a start and a stop. Review yesterday's notes before new material. A short recap in your own words before you close the books.
  • Faith and mindset. A set time of prayer, scripture, or quiet. A fixed number of pages. One honest sentence on paper, kept wherever you already write. The checklist marks that you did it. It does not store the sentence.
  • Work. The one task that moves the job, done before the inbox. A limit on the morning you give to messages. A time when the laptop closes.

Do not copy all four lists into one day. Pick the life you are in this month.

How to cut a list that is too long

Run it for seven days before you add anything. If the same line fails four times, shrink it until it is done or not done. "Gym for an hour" can become "start the session." Raise the bar after the chain exists. Keep the order stable so the first unfinished line is obvious. Changing the order every morning is another way to avoid the hard line.

How REGIME holds the list

REGIME is that checklist. You choose the habits and add your own. The screen shows the next unfinished habit. Hold a habit to check it off, and only after the work is done. When every non-negotiable is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp, and that finished day grows the streak. Miss a day and the streak resets. You can start again today. There is no partial credit.

Eight ranks are earned from the chain, from RECRUIT toward IMMORTAL. They are not something you buy. The weekly review shows the last seven days, which is when you notice the line you keep skipping. Share the milestone if you want. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device.

FAQ

What counts as a daily non-negotiable?
A line the day is incomplete without. If you can skip it and still call the day done, it does not belong on the list. Nice extras can wait.
How many lines should the list have?
Few enough that a bad day still gets finished. Three to six lines is a practical range. Twenty lines is a wish list, and wish lists die on Wednesday.
Are the example lists built into the app?
No. The fitness, study, faith, and work lists on this page are examples you type yourself. REGIME does not ship them as a preset program. Add the lines you will repeat.
How do you mark a non-negotiable done?
Hold the habit to commit it, and only after that piece of the day is done. When every non-negotiable is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp. That finished day grows the streak.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. There is no REGIME account.
