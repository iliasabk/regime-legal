Who should pick soft vs REGIME

Prefer a softer / flexible tracker if you:

  • Need skip days or streak freezes to stay engaged.
  • Want journaling, mood charts, or reflection prompts as the core.
  • Want social accountability or RPG gamification.
  • Want a generous free tier with many habits unlocked.

Prefer REGIME if you:

  • Want the streak to mean “I finished the day.”
  • Keep a short list of non-negotiables and hate Monday rebuilds.
  • Want no account and a verifiable Data Not Collected privacy label.
  • Accept that habit tracking is paid after onboarding (trial on yearly for eligible users).

Neither side is “more moral.” Match the rule to the person.

FAQ

Is REGIME just Streaks with a mean slogan?
No. Streaks-class apps are excellent Apple-native streak tools with their own design and pricing. REGIME’s product center is the non-negotiables day checklist + hard reset + ranks, with a different monetization model. Try both if unsure.
Is REGIME anti-Habitica?
No. Habitica-class gamification helps many people. If you want parties and RPG loops, use that. REGIME is for people who want fewer systems and a stricter day rule.
Why mention “soft” at all?
Because searchers ask for hard mode, discipline, and “miss day reset.” Naming the pattern helps them self-select, without fake competitor bashing.
