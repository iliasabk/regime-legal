75 Soft and 75 Medium are informal community variations and not affiliated with REGIME. People use those names for easier cousins of the 75 day pattern associated with 75 Hard. This page uses the names only to describe those variations. If you want the five tasks Andy Frisella publishes, read the 75 Hard tracker page and follow his list, not a remix.

What usually changes, and where posts disagree

75 Hard, on his rules page, is a fixed daily set: diet with no cheat meals and no alcohol, two workouts with one outdoors, a gallon of water, 10 pages of nonfiction, and a progress photo. A miss restarts that program. Softer write-ups keep the long daily stretch and lower the load, and they disagree with each other. The right-hand column is a pattern of posts, not a standard.

Put that version on the checklist

Each task you kept becomes one non-negotiable with a finish line. "The amount of water I wrote down." "The one session, done." "The pages, read." Do not mix his daily photo, a softer diet, and a second workout unless that mix is the version you chose on purpose. The checklist is the version. Anything you did not write down is not part of today.

Hold each line to commit it after the work. When every line you wrote is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp and the streak grows. A missed day resets the streak. That reset is REGIME's rule, not a 75 day program clock. The app will not measure water, time the session, or keep the photo. Eight ranks, from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL, record the chain. The weekly review shows a fuzzy rule as a skipped line. You do not need an account.

Softer still has to be binary

"Move more" is not a line. "Forty-five minutes, once, before dinner" is. If you cannot tell a miss from a kept day, you do not have a variation. A single 30 day promise is the 30 day challenge tracker. One small rule on a dull day is the self discipline page.

FAQ

Is 75 Soft part of REGIME?
No. 75 Soft and 75 Medium are informal community variations and not affiliated with REGIME. The names describe challenges people post about. They are not a REGIME product.
Are the 75 Soft and 75 Medium rules the same in every post?
No. They vary by source. One post's water target, workout count, or photo rule will not match the next. Write down the version you are actually following.
Will REGIME count water, time a workout, or store a photo?
No. You mark a checklist line after you have done that part of the day. The app does not measure water, time a session, or keep a photo log.
What happens if I miss a day?
REGIME resets the streak. You can start again today. That is the app's rule. It is not a 75 day program clock, and it does not follow someone else's restart policy for you.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. There is no REGIME account.
