People looking for a habit tracker offline, or a private one, or one with no sign up, usually want to tick a box without building a profile. Here is the whole claim, in the App Store wording, and nothing pasted on after it:

No account required. Your habit entries stay on your device. REGIME does not require an account or its own cloud service.

There is no social feed.

What no sign up means on this app

You do not create a REGIME account to keep the checklist. There is no profile to complete before the first line, and no feed of other days sitting next to yours. Private, on this page, means that narrow thing: your entries are not a REGIME account and not a post. The privacy policy is the support page at the root of this site. Read that page for the policy. This guide will not restate it in broader language, and it will not add a promise the App Store text does not make.

On the device, the day is still a checklist

Staying on the device is not a reduced mode. You still keep daily non-negotiables. You still hold a habit to commit it after the work. When every line is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp and the streak grows. Miss a day and the streak resets. You can start again today. Eight ranks run from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL and record that chain. The weekly review is the last seven days. You can share a milestone if you want a picture of a finished day. Sharing is a choice you make. It is not a feed, and it is not required for the day to count.

No sign up also means the first session is the checklist, not a form. You add the lines you mean to keep, you do one, you hold it. Nothing about that sequence asks you to create a profile first. Private here is the absence of that profile and the absence of a feed, not a longer slogan.

That is the product behind the privacy sentences. A 30 day container for one of those lines is the 30 day challenge page. Why the miss should reset the number is why a hard streak. How to decide which lines deserve the stamp is daily non-negotiables.

What this page will not stretch

Offline, private, and no sign up are easy words to inflate. This page stops at the sentences above: no account required, entries on your device, no REGIME cloud service, and no social feed. If a review, a forum post, or another guide says more than that about REGIME, it is not this page. Come back to the privacy policy and the App Store listing. The checklist does not need a sign up either way. Open it, do the line, hold to commit.
