01 / The rule

Soft apps taught you to negotiate. REGIME does not.

You rewrote the week in Notes every Monday. Gym. Deep work. Phone out of bed. By Wednesday half of it was gone, so you rebuilt the list again like that was the work.

The rule here: one short checklist of non-negotiables. Close the list and the day is done. Miss a day and the streak dies. Start again today.

  • Too much to clearTwelve habits. Cosplay. A bad day kills the plan.
  • Too easy to bargainFreeze tokens and streak forgiveness teach you to negotiate every skip.
  • Too much performanceA feed makes you perform instead of finishing.
