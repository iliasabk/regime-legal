A self accountability app is easy to picture as another person who will notice if you slip. REGIME does not add that person. Accountability here is three records you can check with no one else present: the hold that commits a finished line, the streak that makes a miss obvious the next morning, and the weekly review you read alone.

Hold the line after the work

Intending is not a record. Do the thing, then hold the habit to commit it. The hold is deliberate. It is the moment the line changes from open to done. Until you hold it, the line stays open, and an open line cannot become a DAY COMPLETE stamp. You cannot commit the day in the morning and spend the afternoon hoping the hold was close enough.

Write each line so you could score it without a debate. A vague verb leaves room to count a day you only approached. A finish line does not. Choosing those few lines is the work on daily non-negotiables. Keeping one of them on a day when you do not feel like it is the subject of self discipline. This page is about who is allowed to sign the day off. Only you, and only after the hold.

Let the miss stay on the screen

Miss a day and the streak resets. The next morning the number is back at the start. That drop is the accountability. You do not send an explanation to anyone. You see the gap, and you start again today on the same lines. There is no partner whose reply could soften the number, and no group that can mark the day done in your place.

The argument for leaving the gap painful is on why a hard streak. If you want the attendance question by itself, the finished day and the stamp, read the consistency tracker.

Review the week in an empty room

The weekly review is the last seven days. Open it by yourself. Two questions are enough. Which day has no stamp? Which line did you reword so you could still count it? Answer them, then do tomorrow's first open line. There is no meeting on the calendar, no partner waiting for an update, and no group that expects a recap.

A useful review is short. Thursday is empty if Thursday has no stamp. Say so, fix one line if the emptiness repeats, and close the review. The next check is tomorrow's hold.

One optional card, and nothing else leaves

You can make a milestone share card of a finished day and send it if you choose. The card is a picture of one completed checklist. The day is already complete before you make the card. Nobody has to receive it. Sending it does not create a partner, and it does not put you in a group. If you never make the card, the hold, the miss, and the weekly review still work.

Names for a chain you kept alone, from RECRUIT onward, are listed with their day counts on the habit tracker with ranks page. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device.

FAQ

Do I need a partner?
No. There is no partner to add. You answer for the day with the hold, the streak, and the weekly review.
Is there a group?
No. There is no group to join and no recap you owe anyone. The weekly review is the last seven days, and you read it.
What is the milestone share card?
A picture of a finished day you can send if you want. The checklist already counts without it. Sending the card does not add a partner.
How do I commit a habit?
Do the work, then hold the habit to commit it. The hold is the record. Intending to do the line leaves it open.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. There is no REGIME account to create.
