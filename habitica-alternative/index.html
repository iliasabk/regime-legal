Habitica is a habit app that treats real life like a game. Its own site says you track Habits, Dailies, and To Do's, earn experience and gold, level an avatar, and unlock equipment, pets, skills, and quests. Missed Dailies cost health. Parties fight monsters together. A lot of people stay consistent because the game is fun. That is a real design, not a flaw.

It is also the wrong design if the game is the reason you opened a habit tracker and the reason you stopped trusting it. If you want a Habitica alternative with no gamified RPG, REGIME is the narrower tool: a daily checklist, a hard streak, eight ranks, and a weekly review. The ranks are not loot. There is no avatar to dress and no party to disappoint.

What you give up, on purpose

Switching is a loss if you like the game. You will not hatch a pet, take damage, pool a quest, or spend gold on a reward. REGIME is plain on purpose. The Today screen is the chain and the orders for the day. Complete every one, or the day does not count.

The checklist is the planner. The next unfinished habit is the focus. Hold a habit to check it off, and only after you have done it. When every non-negotiable is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp. Optional morning and evening reminders can bracket a morning routine and an evening close. The weekly review shows the last seven days of check-ins. Eight ranks mark the chain.

How to move a routine across

Do not export your whole Habitica life and call that discipline. The long list is often the problem.

  1. From your Dailies, keep only the lines you finish on a normal week. Leave the To Do pile where it is. REGIME is not a task inbox.
  2. Drop anything you were doing for gold, for a quest scroll, or because the party was waiting. If the only reason to floss was a crit, decide whether floss is actually a non-negotiable. If it is, it goes on the checklist. If it is not, leave it off.
  3. Add those few habits, turn off the rest, and keep the order stable. A long imported list is a second character sheet. Do not bring it.
  4. Run one week. Open the weekly review before you add a new line. A chain that survives seven honest days is a better goal than a character that levels while the morning routine slips.
  5. If you miss a day, the streak resets. There is no health potion and no party member to cover you. Start again today with the same short list.

Stay on Habitica if the avatar, the pets, and the party are why you show up. A strict checklist will bore some people, and that is a reason to keep the game, not a reason to insult it. Broader patterns are on REGIME vs soft habit apps.

FAQ

Is REGIME a Habitica clone without the graphics?
No. Habitica is a game built around habits, dailies, to-dos, an avatar, gold, pets, and parties. REGIME is a daily checklist with one hard streak and eight ranks. There is no avatar, no gold, and no party.
Will I lose health points if I miss a day?
REGIME has no health bar. A missed day resets the streak. You start again today.
Can friends quest with me?
No. There is no party and no social feed. You can share a completed-day milestone through the system share sheet when you choose to.
Should I import my whole Habitica list?
Move only the few dailies you will finish. A long list recreates the Monday rebuild REGIME is built to stop. Turn off anything that does not fit.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. Habitica is an online game with a player profile. REGIME is not.
