Search "self discipline app" and you mostly find speeches. The useful object is smaller. Discipline is a rule that still runs on the day the work looks dull. Motivation is the feeling that sometimes arrives after you have started, or on the days the work looks interesting. If you wait to feel ready, the list stays empty and the feeling never has a reason to show up. REGIME is the list and the cost of skipping it. It is not a pep talk.

One rule, before any new identity

The usual Monday move is to become "a disciplined person" by installing twelve habits as proof. The identity is what a boring rule looks like after it has been kept. It is not the starting move. Pick one rule you can do when you are tired, late, and unimpressed with yourself. Put it first on the checklist. Leave the other eleven in a note where they cannot pretend to be today's standard.

Add a second rule only after the first has been dull for a week and you did it anyway. Dull is the test. Interesting is luck. If you need a container of thirty mornings for that single rule, use the 30 day challenge page. If the rule is really "these few lines, or the day did not happen," the cutting work lives on daily non-negotiables. This page is only about the order: small rule first, feeling later, if it comes at all.

The miss has to cost the number

A streak that survives every excuse teaches you the rule was optional. You learn to rename a skip as rest, then as balance, then as a system that understands you. REGIME does the plainer thing. Miss a day and the streak resets. You can start again today. The gap is visible. You do the rule again. That cost is the practice. There is no speech attached to it.

Eight ranks run from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL. They record days kept. They are not a mood score, not a diagnosis, and not something you buy. Hold the habit to commit it only after the work, so a DAY COMPLETE stamp cannot be awarded for intending. If you want the longer argument for why the number should break, read why a hard streak. The calendar story people tell about not breaking a chain is a different page: don't break the chain.

Look at the week, then stop redesigning

The weekly review is the last seven days, without a new system. Which day is empty? Which rule did you reword so you could still count it? Answer that, then do tomorrow's first line. Do not spend the empty evening building a better checklist. On a day with no desire to start, the sequence is short: open the list, do the first unfinished line, hold it, stop. Share a milestone later if you want a record of a kept stretch. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device.

A named strict season, with several rules you chose for a limited run, is monk mode. A softer 75 day variation people post about, which is not a REGIME product, is the 75 Soft page. Neither replaces the first boring rule.

FAQ

Will REGIME make me feel motivated?
No. It holds the rule and resets the streak if you miss the day. Feeling ready is not a step in the checklist.
How many rules should I start with?
One, until it has been dull for a week and you still did it. Add the next rule after that, not before.
What do the ranks mean?
Eight ranks, from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL, record days kept. They are not a mood score and not something you buy.
What is the weekly review for?
The last seven days. Use it to see which rule you renamed so you could count it, and which day you skipped.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. There is no REGIME account.
