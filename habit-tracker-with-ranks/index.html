A habit tracker with ranks is useful when a bare number is hard to remember and a vague badge would be worse. REGIME puts eight names on one streak. Seven of those names have a day count. The eighth name is IMMORTAL, and this page does not give it a day count. If a number is not in the table below, it is not a rank threshold.

The name changes when the streak hits the next count. The checklist stays the list you wrote. A higher rank does not finish the day for you.

RECRUIT is 0 days. That is a chain that has not started. DISCIPLE is 3 kept days. SOLDIER is 7, one week of finished checklists. SPARTAN is 14. GLADIATOR is the rank at 30 days. IMMORTAL is the last name on the ladder, after TITAN. This page does not give it a day count. The seven counts above are the thresholds.

The mornings that are not a new name

Most days sit between the rows. Day 1 and day 2 are still RECRUIT, because DISCIPLE waits at 3. Day 4, day 5, and day 6 are still DISCIPLE. Day 8 through day 13 are still SOLDIER. Day 15 is still SPARTAN. Day 31 is still GLADIATOR. You wear the current name until the streak hits the next count in the table.

Those in-between mornings are the habit. Finish today and the same name is still there tomorrow.

A miss resets the streak

A missed day resets the streak. Start again today with the same checklist.

Why a broken number should stay broken is argued on why a hard streak. The daily attendance habit, apart from these names, is the consistency tracker.

The list does not get easier as the name rises

Hold each habit to commit it after the work is done. When every non-negotiable is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp and the streak grows by one. The weekly review is the last seven days. You can share a milestone if you want a picture of a finished day. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device.

Checking that miss with no one else in the room is the self accountability page. One finished day as a chain, without this table, is don't break the chain. The lines worth putting under a rank in the first place are on daily non-negotiables.

FAQ

What are the eight ranks?
RECRUIT is 0 days, DISCIPLE is 3, SOLDIER is 7, SPARTAN is 14, GLADIATOR is 30, WARLORD is 60, and TITAN is 90. IMMORTAL is the eighth name. This page does not give IMMORTAL a day count.
Does the checklist change at a new rank?
No. The rank names the length of the streak. The lines you have to finish stay the lines you wrote.
What rank applies on the days between counts?
You keep the last count you have reached. Day 8 is still SOLDIER. Day 15 is still SPARTAN. The next name waits for its day count.
What happens to the rank if I miss a day?
A missed day resets the streak. Start again today with the same checklist.
Do I need an account?
No. The ranks sit with the checklist on your device. There is no REGIME account.
