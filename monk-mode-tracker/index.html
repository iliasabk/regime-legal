Monk mode is a name people use for a short, strict season: a set bedtime, training, deep work before the day fills up, reading, and food you decided in advance. There is no single official rulebook. If someone sells you one, that is their program. Your rules are the ones you can still do next Tuesday.

REGIME does not lock the phone

A lot of monk mode searches are searches for a lock. REGIME does not block apps and it does not limit screen time. It will not hide other apps or cut you off at a minute count. If you want blocking, pair the checklist with your phone's built-in Screen Time and turn that on yourself. Screen Time is the phone's tool. REGIME is the list of rules. This app does not connect to Screen Time for you.

Use both if you need both. The checklist will not control the rest of the phone.

A schedule that can survive week three

Write the day as a handful of lines, in order. This shape is an example, not a preset inside the app.

  1. Up at the time you wrote down.
  2. Train before the inbox. One session, defined in advance.
  3. One deep-work sitting, with a start and a stop, on the task that matters.
  4. Read a set number of pages.
  5. Lights out at the time you wrote down.

That is already a full day. A food rule or a walk can replace a line. Do not stack them all on top until the short list has survived a week. "Become a monk" is not a line you can check.

Pick the length yourself. REGIME does not run a clock that ends the season for you. Lines outside a named season are on the daily non-negotiables page.

How the checklist marks the day

Put the rules on the daily checklist. The screen shows the next unfinished habit. Hold a habit to commit it, and only after that rule is done. When every rule is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp and the streak grows. Miss a day and the streak resets. You can start again today.

Eight ranks are earned from that chain, from RECRUIT toward IMMORTAL. They record consistency. They are not something you buy. The weekly review shows the last seven days, which is when you see the rule you keep negotiating away. Share a milestone if you want. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device.

FAQ

Does REGIME block apps or limit screen time?
No. REGIME does not block apps and it does not limit screen time. If you want the phone locked down, use the built-in Screen Time settings on the phone. REGIME stays the checklist for the rules you chose.
What is monk mode, in plain terms?
A name people use for a short season of strict personal rules: sleep, training, deep work, reading, and fewer distractions. It is not an official program with a single published rulebook. You write the rules. The tracker only records whether you kept them today.
What should a monk mode schedule include?
A few lines you will still do on day 20. A fixed wake time, one training session, one deep-work sitting, reading, and a lights-out time is already a full day. Add a line only after the shorter list survives a week.
How does the day get marked complete?
Hold each habit to commit it after the work is done. When every rule on the list is done, the day gets a DAY COMPLETE stamp and the streak grows. A missed day resets the streak. You can start again today.
Do I need an account?
No. Habit entries stay on your device. There is no REGIME account. The season is yours to keep, not a score you post for an audience.
