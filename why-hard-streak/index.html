The Monday rebuild problem

You know the pattern. Sunday night conviction. Monday checklist of twenty habits. Wednesday collapse. Friday “new system.” The app did not fail because you lack motivation graphics. It failed because the routine was optional theater.

REGIME’s answer is narrow on purpose:

  • One checklist of non-negotiables, the few things that define the day.
  • Visible next unfinished item.
  • Consistency tracked as a streak and ranks you earn.
  • No social feed to perform for.

REGIME progresses you through eight ranks as you stay consistent. Ranks are not cosmetics you buy and not RPG loot. They mark that the chain held.
