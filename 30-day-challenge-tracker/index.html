A 30 day challenge tracker fails when the promise changes shape. Day 1 is a speech. Day 14 is a smaller, quieter task you hope still counts. This page is the order of work: choose one idea, make day 1 finishable, treat days 10 to 20 as the real test, and use day 30 to decide what happens next. REGIME does not hand you a themed month. You type the line.

Pick one idea, then stop shopping

Choose a single daily act you could still do on a travel day. If the honest answer is no, it is a week-long stunt with a 30 day label. Ideas that survive that test:

  • Two hundred words, finished, before you open messages.
  • A walk of a distance you wrote down, after the last meeting.
  • No alcohol, scored yes or no, with no "special occasion" clause.
  • One language lesson completed, not merely opened.
  • The same home strength set, same reps, even when the gym is closed.
  • Every purchase written down before the next one is made.

Take one. A second challenge is how week two gets negotiated away. A longer life list is a different job, on the daily non-negotiables page.

Day 1: write a line a stranger could score

Put the challenge on the checklist in words that do not depend on your mood. "Be consistent" cannot be scored. "Lesson finished" can. Do the work first. Then hold the habit to commit it. The hold is the confirmation, not a tap you give yourself for planning. If the day ends with a DAY COMPLETE stamp, the definition was small enough. If day 1 does not finish, shrink the line before day 2. Do not collect six unfinished days and call that data.

Days 10 to 20: the part the idea list skips

By the second week the novelty is gone. Open the weekly review before you rewrite the challenge. Two empty days means the line is too large, or it sits at an hour you never have. Fix the size or the hour. Do not add a bonus task to make the month feel serious again.

A missed day resets the streak. Start the next morning with the same definition. Restarting is allowed. Pretending the gap still counts is not. Why that reset should be felt is on why a hard streak, and the chain method is on don't break the chain.

Day 30: the rank, then a decision

If the chain is intact, the rank at 30 days is GLADIATOR. It sits on the ladder from RECRUIT to IMMORTAL. Eight ranks record time kept. You do not buy them. Share the milestone if you want, then choose: keep the line, stop, or write a new definition. An ending you chose is not a miss.

There is no separate 30 day clock. The streak is the record. Hold to commit after the work. You do not need an account. Habit entries stay on your device. Keeping a rule when you do not feel like it is the self discipline page. A stricter season you name yourself is monk mode.

FAQ

One. A second promise is how day 12 gets bargained away. Finish or restart the first line before you add another.
What do I do on day 1?

What if I miss a day in the middle?
The streak resets. Start again today with the same definition. REGIME does not keep a separate 30 day clock that ignores the miss.
